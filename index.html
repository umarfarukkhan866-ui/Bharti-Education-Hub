🎯 Exam Preparation

Complete exam preparation system

📅 Preparation Plan

  1. Complete every chapter concept.
  2. Read chapter notes carefully.
  3. Practice textbook questions.
  4. Solve question-bank questions.
  5. Attempt chapter quizzes.
  6. Revise formulas and definitions.
  7. Attempt a mock test.
  8. Check mistakes and revise weak topics.

➗ Maths Exam Tips

  • Learn formulas properly.
  • Show every calculation step.
  • Practice numerical questions.
  • Check units in word problems.

🔬 Science Exam Tips

  • Understand concepts.
  • Learn definitions and terms.
  • Practice diagrams.
  • Revise experiments.

🌍 SST Exam Tips

  • Learn important dates and events.
  • Understand maps and locations.
  • Revise definitions.
  • Write answers in points.

📖 Hindi & English

  • Revise grammar rules.
  • Practice writing.
  • Read chapters carefully.
  • Practice unseen passages.
